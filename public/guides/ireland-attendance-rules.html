Attendance rules for English language schools in Ireland

Short answer: schools listed on ILEP, and schools authorised to use the TrustEd Ireland mark, must record attendance in every class, apply the 15-minute rule, act when a student has 25% or more uncertified absence in the first six weeks, and track each student against the 85% attendance requirement. Inspections can be unannounced.

The rules in one list

  • 15-minute rule: arriving more than 15 minutes late, or leaving before the class ends, is marked absent for that class or segment.
  • Class teacher's student count: recorded next to the register.
  • First six weeks: students with 25% or more uncertified absence need attention early.
  • 85%: the attendance level each student is expected to reach, so the useful question is who can no longer reach it.
  • Records: the full attendance record should be ready to show at an inspection or when ISD asks.

Where the rules come from

  • "TrustEd Ireland Scheme and Immigration Requirements: Information for Education Providers", section "Recording of attendance for English Language Programmes".
  • ILEP criteria for English language programmes (March 2025), section "Monitoring, Inspections and Investigations", which allows unannounced inspections at any time.

This page summarises published requirements to help you prepare. It is not legal advice. Always read the current official text.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 15-minute rule?

A student who arrives more than 15 minutes late, or leaves before the class ends, is marked absent for that class or segment.

What happens at 25% uncertified absence in the first six weeks?

Eightyfive flags students with 25% or more uncertified absence in the first six weeks so the school can act early. The exact obligation is set out in the published TrustEd Ireland requirements, which the school should read in their current version.

What is the 85% attendance requirement?

Students are expected to reach 85% attendance. Eightyfive forecasts each student's attendance against 85% so the school knows early who can no longer reach it.

Can inspections be unannounced?

Yes. The ILEP criteria for English language programmes (March 2025), section Monitoring, Inspections and Investigations, allow unannounced inspections at any time.

Does Eightyfive guarantee we pass an inspection?

No tool can promise that. Eightyfive keeps the attendance record complete and easy to show, but compliance stays with the school.
