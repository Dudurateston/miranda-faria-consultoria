ILEP and TrustEd attendance rules: the 85% requirement explained

Short answer: the TrustEd Ireland requirements say English language providers must have mandatory attendance requirements of a minimum 85% attendance for all enrolled students, and students must demonstrate at least 85% attendance for their immigration permission. Attendance is recorded in every class, and a student who arrives more than 15 minutes late or leaves before the class ends is marked absent.

What the 85% requirement says

The scheme document sets out the 85% rule in two places. Providers "must have mandatory attendance requirements of a minimum 85% attendance for all enrolled students", and students "must progress in their studies and demonstrate at least 85% attendance". Providers still listed on ILEP must keep complying with the relevant ILEP criteria until they are authorised to use the TrustEd Ireland mark or the listing ends.

How attendance is defined

  • 15-minute rule: attending means the full designated tuition hours. Arriving more than 15 minutes late, or leaving before the class has concluded, means the student is marked absent for that class or class segment.
  • No routine leeway: the 15 minutes must not become a way to let students arrive late regularly without being marked absent.
  • Every class: attendance is recorded in each class, and the class teacher also records the overall number of students in attendance.
  • Documented system: providers need a clear system, with the nominated responsible people and the rules on punctuality and penalties for lateness, documented and publicly available.
  • Records: manual attendance sheets must be kept for at least 12 months and are checked against computer records. The records must be available for inspection by Immigration Service Delivery (ISD).

The first six weeks and the 85% line

  • If a student has 25% or more uncertified absence in the first six weeks, the provider must communicate this to ISD, together with the attendance policy being applied and the measures taken to address non-attendance.
  • If a student cannot make up attendance to 85% before the programme ends, the student must be told they do not meet the requirement, and this must be communicated to ISD.
  • The document states that there is no mechanism for students to make up uncertified absences through additional classes, during or after the programme.

Illustrative arithmetic

Illustration only, with invented numbers. It is not an official calculation method. Which unit attendance is measured in must follow the provider's documented policy.

Suppose a student is timetabled for 100 class hours in a period. At 85% attendance the student attends at least 85 of them, so no more than 15 hours can be missed. Once the missed hours pass 15, the student can no longer reach 85% in that period, and in practice the school needs to know that early, not at the end.

Inspections

The document says Immigration Service Delivery (ISD), in the Department of Justice, Home Affairs and Migration, reserves the right to conduct unannounced inspections, spot checks and monitoring of providers at any time. Attendance monitoring records are among the evidence they may ask for.

Frequently asked questions

What is the 85% attendance requirement?

English language providers must have mandatory attendance requirements of a minimum 85% attendance for all enrolled students, and students must demonstrate at least 85% attendance, according to the TrustEd Ireland scheme and immigration requirements document.

What is the 15-minute rule?

A student who arrives more than 15 minutes late, or leaves before the class has concluded, is marked absent for that class or class segment. The leeway must not become a way to allow regular lateness.

What happens at 25% uncertified absence in the first six weeks?

The provider must communicate it to ISD, setting out the attendance policy applied and the measures taken to address non-attendance.

Can a student make up missed attendance with extra classes?

No. The document states that students cannot make up uncertified absences through additional classes, either during or after the programme.

Can inspections be unannounced?

Yes. The document says unannounced inspections, spot checks and monitoring can take place.

Does Eightyfive guarantee we pass an inspection?

No tool can promise that. Eightyfive keeps the attendance record complete and easy to show, but compliance stays with the school.
