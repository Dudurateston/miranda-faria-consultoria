Miranda Faria: fact sheet

Short answer: Miranda Faria is a software and design studio run by Eduardo Miranda Faria in Piumhi, Minas Gerais, Brazil. It builds custom business systems, websites, brand identity and AI automation, and has two products: Apontador (time-clock and absenteeism analysis for factories in Brazil) and Eightyfive (attendance records for English language schools in Ireland).

Services

  • Systems and management: custom management systems (stock, sales, attendance, KPIs).
  • Development: websites, web systems and apps.
  • Design: brand identity and interface design.
  • Automation: AI and WhatsApp automation of repetitive work.

Products

  • Eightyfive: attendance register for English language schools in Ireland listed on ILEP or authorised to use the TrustEd Ireland mark. Guide: attendance rules.
  • Apontador (in Portuguese): reads the AFD file of Brazilian time clocks (Portaria MTP 671/2021) and shows absenteeism by day, sector and month, overtime and its cost, and labour-law (CLT) points of attention. Guide: what the AFD is.

How a project runs

  1. Diagnosis: understand the problem and what it costs before talking price or technology (the site has a free cost diagnosis).
  2. A working demo in the first week.
  3. Delivery in the owner's hands: the system is handed over in the client's name, and the client keeps the data and access.
